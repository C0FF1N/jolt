Let your AI use your real browser.

Claude Code, VS Code and any MCP client drive the one tab you choose. Already logged in, always visible, entirely on your machine.

Zero setup

Claude Code and VS Code. At once.

Each client gets its own server and Jolt finds them all. Pick one, press connect, and you are done.

Self-healing

It reconnects by itself.

Restart your client and Jolt releases the tab, finds it again within about a second, and carries on. No clicking.

Never in the dark

Watch every click.

A cursor glides to each action so you always see what the AI is doing. One switch turns it off.
